About Us

Our Story, Our Legacy

Established in 2020, SPC OBU New Zealand is the official chapter of the St. Peter's College Old Boys' Union for Peterites who call Aotearoa home.

Est. 2020

From Colombo 04 to Aotearoa

St. Peter's College, Colombo 04, has shaped generations of young men since 1922 under the motto Virtus et Veritas - Virtue and Truth. As Peterites made new lives across the world, the bonds of College followed them.

In 2020, a group of old boys in New Zealand came together to formally establish SPC OBU New Zealand - creating a home for the growing Peterite community across the country. What began as reunions among friends has grown into a thriving union hosting the Gala Dinner Dance, the Joe-Pete NZ Battle of the Saints, family events, and charitable initiatives supporting our alma mater.

Today, we proudly stand alongside our mother OBU in Sri Lanka and sister branches worldwide - one global brotherhood, united by the blue, white and gold.

Our Mission

To unite, celebrate and serve the Peterite community in New Zealand

We exist to keep old boys connected to one another and to St. Peter's College - fostering fellowship, supporting members and their families, and contributing to causes that reflect the values our College instilled in us.

Brotherhood

The bonds formed at College are for life. We look after our own - through celebration and challenge alike.

Legacy

We honour the traditions of St. Peter's College and pass the Peterite spirit to the next generation in New Zealand.

Service

Virtus et Veritas calls us to give back - to our College, our communities, and those in need.

Community

From Auckland to Christchurch, we create spaces where Peterite families connect and belong.

One Global Family

Connected to the mother OBU in Sri Lanka

SPC OBU New Zealand operates in close partnership with the St. Peter's College Old Boys' Union in Colombo - the mother union that has served the College for over a century. Our Patron, Rev. Fr. Rohitha Rodrigo, Rector of St. Peter's College, and Vice Patron Asoka Attapattu, current President of the Sri Lanka OBU, embody that living connection.

Together with branches across Australia, the UK, Canada and beyond, we form a global network of Peterites supporting the College and each other, wherever life has taken us.

The Rivalry

Ninety years of cricket, one unbreakable bond

Since 1933, the annual "Battle of the Saints" between St. Joseph's College and St. Peter's College has been one of Sri Lanka's most beloved sporting traditions - two brother schools, one founder's legacy, and a rivalry played in the finest spirit of the game.

Joe-Pete NZ brings that tradition to New Zealand. Each year, Josephians and Peterites across Aotearoa come together for a day of cricket, family festivities and friendship - proof that the Battle of the Saints is less about who wins, and more about what we share.
